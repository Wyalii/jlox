# Intro

This idea came a bit late in the process, but I decided to journal my work on this interpreter and compiler project using _Crafting Interpreters_.

> [!NOTE]
> **Why I am keeping this journal:** To track what I think, learn, and experience along the way and to give myself an easy entry point back into the code after busy university days.

## Motivation & University Balance

My main motivation is to build something cool that I can be proud of. Balancing this with university is not always easy, especially after long hours of lectures, so I am doing my best to show up consistently even in small sessions.

The hardest part for me not just here, but in general is getting over the **starting friction** and sitting down to begin. Once I start, it feels great. Writing a quick journal entry is my way of lowering that first-step barrier and warming up my brain.

## What You Will Find Here

- **Book & Code Summaries:** What I read in _Crafting Interpreters_, what I implemented, and how I made sense of the ideas.
- **Interesting Takeaways & Bugs:** Concepts that clicked, edge cases that tripped me up, and lessons learned.
- **Better Writing & Markdown Skills:** Practicing clear English writing and getting comfortable with `.md` files along the way.

## Looking Ahead

I am starting this intro here because I want a clear baseline as I revisit earlier parts of the book and keep moving forward. If this habit sticks, I plan to journal future projects too and eventually turn them into a portfolio-style collection or online book showcasing how I build and learn.
